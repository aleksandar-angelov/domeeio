Domeeio

Resident App · Hi-fi Prototype

a warmer building, together

Standardized channel for residents of a 400-unit building in Skopje — issue cases, group chat, polls, bills, calendar, directory, and a Мајстор (craftsman) marketplace. Macedonian primary, English toggle. Built for a single-building pilot, designed to scale multi-tenant.

Cases
Report problems with category, photo, location. Tracked with status timeline.
Polls
Non-binding polls with live result bars, quorum %, and vote persistence.
Chat
Building-wide group chat. President is badged so official voice is clear.
Documents
Bills, meeting minutes, house rules. Unpaid bill hero card on top.
Calendar
Month grid + event list for maintenance, meetings, cleaning.
Directory
Residents by unit/floor with direct message. President tagged.
Мајстор
Book a vetted plumber, electrician or handyman. Per-city, independent role.
Bilingual
Full Macedonian + English. Language toggle in Menu and Tweaks.
